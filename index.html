GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick answers.

Are all of these resources free?+

Browsing this site is always free. Many listed services are free to ELAC students. Others depend on eligibility, available funding, or supplies. Health services may require the student health fee, and childcare, course enrollment, or club participation may have costs. Every listing includes an access note.

Do I need to make an account?+

No account is needed here. Save resources using the bookmark button; your list stays in this browser on this device. It does not sync between devices. Official campus services may require your student login or an application.

How current is the information?+

Our initial directory was researched on October 27, 2026 using official ELAC pages and ELAC library guides. Details can change. Use the official link in each listing for current hours, eligibility, deadlines, and availability. This is a growing guide, not a guarantee that every campus service is included.

How can I suggest a resource or report an update?+

Use our GitHub issue form to suggest an addition or correction. Include a public official source link. Please do not post student IDs, financial records, medical information, or other private details.

TRANSPARENCY

Sources & privacy

Each listing links to its source: an official ELAC page, ELAC library guide, or the ELAC Reading & Writing Center. Researched October 27, 2026. Information is not automatically updated.

This site has no student accounts, advertising, analytics, or application forms. Saved resource IDs are kept in your browser’s local storage. Your browser may prevent or clear that storage. External websites have their own privacy policies.

This independent directory does not determine eligibility or provide professional advice. Contact the relevant campus office to confirm services.
